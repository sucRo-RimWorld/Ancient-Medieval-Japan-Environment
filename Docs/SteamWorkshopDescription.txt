[h1]Ancient & Medieval Japan - Environment（中世日本 - 環境）[/h1]

[b]Reconfigures RimWorld's natural landscape for medieval Japan.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adjust temperature, rainfall, elevation and hilliness; reweight rain, fog, thunderstorms and snow by climate zone and wetland
[*]In 0.3.0 development: make rain more likely in Tsuyu/Akisame and clear skies more likely afterward; reduce gameplay daylight in rain, storms and fog, affecting plant growth and solar power
[*]Increase small and medium rivers while suppressing natural huge rivers; make coasts more irregular with bays, peninsulas and islands
[*]Add four biomes: warm-temperate forest, cool-temperate forest, subalpine forest and alpine zone
[*]Add Thin Soil (50% fertility) and support Growing Zones for suitable crops; higher elevations retain more stony/poor soil
[*]Add four plants: Sudajii, Japanese beech, Shirabiso and Haimatsu; correct their cut-wood yields
[*]Remove unsuitable plants, adjust vegetation and region-based tree sowing; Haimatsu cannot be sown
[*]Keep TemperateSwamp and ColdBog, add reed, sedges and alder to both and sphagnum to the cool bog
[*]Rebalance wildlife proxies and remove unsuitable wetland pack animals; 0.3.0 development also excludes four unsuitable MO large animals from six Vanilla biome pools
[*]Reed and sedges yield Hay (base 2 and 1), alder yields 24 wood, and sphagnum yields no item
[*]Adjust wetland disease types and intervals; exclude mechanites
[*]Revise descriptions of both wetlands and six retained Vanilla trees with medieval-Japan information
[/list]

[h2]Scope[/h2]
RimWorld 1.6 / 1.7, Beta. English / Japanese.

[h2]日本語 / Japanese[/h2]

[b]地形・気候・植生・動物・バイオームを中世日本風に再構成。[/b]

[h2]主な特徴[/h2]
[list]
[*]気温・降水量・標高・丘陵・山地の分布と、気候帯・湿地ごとの天候（雨・霧・雷雨・積雪）を調整
[*]0.3.0開発版では梅雨・秋雨に雨が選ばれやすくなり、その後は晴天が選ばれやすくなる。雨・雷雨・霧の日照低下が植物生育と太陽光発電に影響
[*]小～中規模の河川を増やし、巨大河川の自然発生を抑制。湾・半島・島が多い複雑な海岸線へ変更
[*]暖温帯林・冷温帯林・亜高山帯林・高山帯の4バイオームを追加
[*]痩せた土壌（肥沃度50%）を追加。対応作物の農業ゾーンも設置可能にし、高地ほど礫地・痩せた土が増えるよう調整
[*]スダジイ・ブナ・シラビソ・ハイマツの4植物を追加し、伐採時の木材取得を調整
[*]不適切な植生を除外し、植生分布と植林候補を調整。ハイマツは植林不可
[*]温帯湿地・冷涼湿原にアシ・スゲ・ハンノキを追加し、冷涼湿原にはミズゴケを追加
[*]野生動物の分布を調整し、湿地から不自然な荷役動物を除外。0.3.0開発版ではMO大型動物4種をバニラ6バイオームから除外
[*]アシは干し草2、スゲは干し草1、ハンノキは木材24を基本収量とし、ミズゴケは採取物なし
[*]湿地の病気の種類・発生間隔を調整し、機械虫病を除外
[*]既存バニラ樹木6種と湿地2種の説明文を、中世日本に関する情報を含めて修正
[/list]

[h2]Representative Trees / 代表樹木[/h2]
Sudajii / スダジイ — Evergreen broadleaf of warm-temperate forests; historically used for nuts and wood. / 暖温帯の常緑広葉樹。種子と木材が古くから利用された。
Japanese beech / ブナ — Deciduous tree of cool-temperate forests; wood used in turned objects. / 冷温帯の落葉高木。木材は挽物などに利用された。
Shirabiso / シラビソ — Evergreen conifer of subalpine forests in Honshu. / 本州の亜高山帯に生える常緑針葉樹。
Haimatsu / ハイマツ — Low, creeping pine of the alpine zone above the treeline. / 森林限界より高い場所に生える、地面を這う低木性のマツ。
