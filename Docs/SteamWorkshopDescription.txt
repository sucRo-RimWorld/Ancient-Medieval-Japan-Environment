[h1]Ancient & Medieval Japan - Environment（中世日本 - 環境）[/h1]

[b]A standalone reconfiguration of RimWorld's terrain, climate, vegetation and wildlife for pre-Edo Japan.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adjust temperature, rainfall, elevation and hilliness
[*]More small/medium rivers; suppress natural huge rivers
[*]Increase coastal bays, peninsulas and islands
[*]Add four biomes: warm/cool temperate, subalpine, alpine
[*]Add four plants: Sudajii, beech, Shirabiso and Haimatsu
[*]Remove unsuitable Vanilla vegetation; rebalance tree/grass shares
[*]Restrict tree-sowing choices by regional distribution; Haimatsu unsowable
[*]Add Thin Soil with 50% fertility
[*]Increase stony/poor natural soil at high elevation
[*]Reweight rain, fog, storms and snow by climate zone; reuse Vanilla seasons
[*]Rebalance wildlife with selected Vanilla animal proxies
[*]Retain two Vanilla wetlands; replace unsuitable wild-plant pools
[*]Rebuild wetland wildlife; exclude unsuitable pack animals
[*]Rebalance wetland disease types and intervals; exclude mechanites
[*]Reweight wetland rain, fog, thunderstorms and snow
[*]Keep Vanilla wetland mud, marsh, shallow water and movement difficulty
[*]Rewrite descriptions of both wetlands and six retained Vanilla trees
[*]Fix native wood yield on cutting four representative plants
[/list]

[h2]Future candidates[/h2]

Vanilla world generation is reused; special biomes including Medieval Overhaul's Dark Forest can coexist. Existing Healroot and herbal medicine remain; medicinal plant systems belong elsewhere.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[b]Required:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url].
[b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]CCTO[/url] (plant cold growth/death/dormancy) and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2553700067]Medieval Overhaul[/url].

[h2]Save compatibility[/h2]
[b]New game recommended[/b] for world, river, terrain and biome changes. Adding to old saves is unverified; the world is not rebuilt. Runtime temperature may change. [b]Do not remove AMJE[/b] from saves using its biomes, terrain or plants.

Beta. Details: [url=https://github.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment]GitHub README[/url]

[h2]日本語 / Japanese[/h2]

[b]バニラの地形・気候・植生・動物を江戸以前の日本をイメージした自然環境へ再構成する独立Mod。[/b]

[h2]主な特徴[/h2]
[list]
[*]気温・降水量・標高・丘陵・山地の分布を変更
[*]小～中規模の河川を増やし、巨大河川の自然発生を抑制
[*]湾・半島・島が多い複雑な海岸線へ変更
[*]暖温帯林・冷温帯林・亜高山帯林・高山帯の4バイオームを追加
[*]スダジイ・ブナ・シラビソ・ハイマツの4植物を追加
[*]不適切なバニラ植生を除外し、樹木・草本の出現比率を再配分
[*]植林候補を地域の自然分布に合わせ、ハイマツは植林不可
[*]痩せた土壌（肥沃度50%）を追加
[*]高地ほど礫地・痩せた土が多くなるよう土壌分布を調整
[*]気候帯別に雨・霧・雷雨・雪を調整し、バニラの季節変化を利用
[*]バニラ動物を代理として、野生動物の出現構成を調整
[*]温帯湿地・冷涼湿原を維持し、不適切な植物構成を修正
[*]湿地の野生動物を変更し、不適切な荷役動物を除外
[*]湿地の病気の種類・発生間隔を変更し、機械虫病を除外
[*]湿地の雨・霧・雷雨・積雪の出現傾向を調整
[*]湿地の泥・沼・浅瀬と移動困難度はバニラを維持
[*]湿地2種と既存バニラ樹木6種の説明文を日英修正
[*]代表植物4種の伐採時の木材取得を修正
[/list]

既存の世界生成を活用し、Medieval OverhaulのDark Forest等と共存可能。既存Healrootと医薬品供給は維持し、薬用植物の機能は別Modの範囲。

[h2]対応・互換性[/h2]
[b]必須Mod:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url]。
[b]任意Mod:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]CCTO[/url]（植物の寒冷耐性）、[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2553700067]Medieval Overhaul[/url]。

[h2]セーブ互換性[/h2]
世界・河川・地形・バイオームの変更には[b]新規ゲームを推奨[/b]。既存セーブへの追加は未検証で、生成済みワールドは変わらない。温度の実行時処理は影響する可能性がある。独自バイオーム・地形・植物を使うセーブからの[b]途中削除は非推奨[/b]。

β版。詳細：[url=https://github.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment]GitHub README[/url]

[h2]Representative Plants / 各環境帯の代表植物[/h2]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/main/Textures/Things/Plant/AMJ/Shii/Shii_A.png[/img]
[b]Warm-temperate — Sudajii / 暖温帯林 — スダジイ[/b]
Evergreen broadleaf tree of southwestern Japan; its nuts and wood have long been used. / 西南日本の照葉樹林を代表する常緑高木。種子や木材は古くから利用されてきました。

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/main/Textures/Things/Plant/AMJ/Beech/Beech_A.png[/img]
[b]Cool-temperate — Japanese beech / 冷温帯林 — ブナ[/b]
A deciduous tree typical of cool forests; its wood was used in turned objects. / 冷温帯を代表する落葉高木。木材は挽物などに利用されました。

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/main/Textures/Things/Plant/AMJ/Shirabiso/Shirabiso_A.png[/img]
[b]Subalpine — Shirabiso / 亜高山帯林 — シラビソ[/b]
An evergreen conifer of Honshu's high mountains. / 本州の高山域に見られる常緑針葉樹です。

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/main/Textures/Things/Plant/AMJ/Haimatsu/Haimatsu_A.png[/img]
[b]Alpine — Haimatsu / 高山帯 — ハイマツ[/b]
Low creeping pine above the treeline. / 森林限界より高所で地面を這うように育つ低木性のマツです。
