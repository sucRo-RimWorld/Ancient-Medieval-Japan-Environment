# AMJ Environment rainy-season prior-art and weather-system audit - 2026-10-21

Scope: Environment standalone, RimWorld 1.6 production target and planned 1.7. Japan-inspired ordinary procedural worlds only. No WorldMap geographic changes. This note supports a bounded initial feature, not final climate realism or release approval.

## Actual current AMJ and Vanilla/MO behavior
- AMJ Environment Source/AncientMedievalJapanEnvironment/WorldGenStep_AMJEnvironmentTerrain.cs adjusts *annual* tile rainfall, biome and climate at world generation. That annual rainfall is also consumed by downstream river generation; rainy-season work must not silently change it.
- AMJ Environment Defs/BiomeDefs/AMJ_Biomes.xml assigns fixed baseWeatherCommonalities across four owned biomes, using existing Vanilla WeatherDef identities (Clear, Fog, Rain, FoggyRain, RainyThunderstorm etc.). Wetland Vanilla biomes are retained. No native intra-year selection exists.
- Vanilla 1.6 code reference (older public decompile, exact live 1.6/1.7 source not asserted): https://github.com/josh-m/RW-Decompile/blob/master/RimWorld/WeatherDecider.cs . WeatherDecider.CurrentWeatherCommonality uses biome.commonalities, rainfall factor, weather temperature range, event rain prevention and fire danger; changes to zero commonality must never be revived. Changing commonality at selection-time, after vanilla disqualification, avoids shared BiomeDef mutation.
- Plant.cs references GrowthRateFactor_Light and GlowGrid.GameGlowAt; regular weather's sky visuals do not automatically prove crop sunlight loss. In this initial slice, no extra growth-rate penalty, solar power modification, or humidity stat is implemented. Rainy-season gameplay and light effects require separate controlled calibration.
- MO is a first-class co-loading target but owns other biomes and plant bundles; this implementation modifies neither MO defs nor its weather definitions, and restricts its effect to AMJ biome identities and retained Vanilla wetland identities. No joint native test has yet been run.

## VE content comparison
- Vanilla Events Expanded Workshop 1938420742: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1938420742 ; weather-related events, drought and disasters, not evidence of Japan's recurring June/July and September/October near-daily weather selection. It introduces substantial events outside Environment's narrow climate scope. Content-Mod VE policy is prior-art only. Detailed current deployed assemblies were not inspected; not assumed interchangeable.
- No VE content pack from the bounded weather/event discovery supplies a verified lightweight Japan-specific calendar-window weather controller. The absence of a universal market-wide equivalent is not claimed.

## Non-VE comparisons
- Seasonal Weather (Continued), Workshop 2045114229, https://github.com/emipa606/SeasonalWeather , source examined on 2026-10-21: Source/SeasonalWeather/SeasonalWeatherExtension.cs mutates map.Biome.baseWeatherCommonalities on season transitions; DateNotifier/GameComponentUtility patches trigger it; Patches/SeasonalWeatherPatches.xml enumerates broad biome/season weather tables. Feature also adds dust, storms, disaster conditions, moods, rain growth +20%. This is reusable design knowledge but changes the global shared biome Def collection, only tracks broad seasons, and creates more features than the AMJ target. Not a mandatory dependency or a substitute for sub-quadrum rainy windows. Licensed MIT; do not copy code.
- WeatherControl, Workshop 3047040031, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3047040031 ; end-user weather weights/durations configurator. Does not establish automatically recurring two Japan-specific season windows; its source/distributed DLL were not inspected. Prior-art only.
- Planetsmith, Workshop 3773634615, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773634615 ; climate circulation/worldgen monsoon/rain-shadow model, a different ownership level with global worldgen replacement. Not suitable to replace Environment's approved terrain/river generation; no native co-load verification.
- Dynamic Atmosphere, Workshop 3808191148, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3808191148 ; optional visual/cloud circulation, some real hurricane/lightning game conditions. Keep base WeatherDef names unchanged so weather remains observable; no claimed DLL co-load PASS or assumption that rendered fronts follow seasonal selections.

## Physical calendar rationale and specific boundary
- JMA historical normals: https://www.data.jma.go.jp/cpd/baiu/kako_baiu09 ; Kanto/Koshin rainy season normally approximately June 7 to July 19. Other regions vary and are NOT distinguished within the four broad AMJ biomes.
- JMA early October 2026 observed Sep rains/sun deficit: https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/press/2610/01a/tenko202610.html . Autumn rain roughly Sep-Oct varies. This is not historical-medieval observation.
- RimWorld's 60-day game year is a compression. Historical first prototype windows day-of-year 15-23 (summer rainy season) and 30-38 (autumn rain) under northern-calendar conversion. Both date windows and multiplier weights are initial gameplay guesses, *not empirically validated probabilities or final approved climate values*. Shift by half a game-year for native Southern Hemisphere tiles; do not globally change seasons, GenDate or WorldMap latitude.

## Architecture and risk decision
- Independent *minimal* WeatherDecider.CurrentWeatherCommonality postfix for AMJ-selected BiomeDefs, adjusting strictly positive Vanilla-weight results only. XML cannot express short in-season temporal weights. No WeatherDef replacement, forced weather, world annual-rainfall changes, WeatherManager mutation, sky shaders or custom game conditions. Unknown third-party WeatherDefs remain untouched. No heavy per-tick sampling: hook runs on weather selection, typically not on every pawn tick.
- Sub-quadrum weighting: smaller thunderstorm boost than rain/fog; slightly favor wet-to-wet sequences without making storms permanent. Natural Vanilla weather constraints remain authoritative. Do not patch other biome providers' weather pools.
- Calibration required before public release: actual daylight vs crop growth/solar; annual weather day fractions, wet-sequence lengths, no extreme drought compensation; forecast and restored save; 1.6 / 1.7, MO/CCTO and Dynamic Atmosphere optional if available; runtime error and performance inspection. Existing reference counts cannot be treated as PASS.
- No content is copied from prior mods. The independent gap is a narrowly scoped intra-season Japan rain-pattern controller that retains established AMJ annual climate and standalone operation.

**Superseding calendar decision, 2026-10-22:** Environment 0.3.0 subsequently revised Tsuyu to days **16–22**, post-Tsuyu fair weather to **23–29**, retained Akisame **30–38** pending rainfall observations, and limited autumn fair weather to **39–44** (winter begins day 45). Formal current windows and meteorological rationale are in owner `Docs/Design.md` section 4.1. The previous 15–23 dates above are retained as an original audit snapshot, not the current production specification.
