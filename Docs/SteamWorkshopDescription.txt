[h1]Ancient & Medieval Japan: Environment[/h1]

[b]An environment overhaul that reshapes RimWorld world generation toward a Japan-like climate, landscape, and vegetation profile.[/b]

It creates an ancient-to-medieval-Japan-inspired environment with a humid climate, mountainous terrain, frequent short rivers, complex coastlines, temperature-based forest belts, and alpine zones.

Ancient & Medieval Japan Core is not required. [b]If you only want a medieval-Japan-like natural environment, this mod can be used on its own.[/b]

[h2]Main Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Japan-oriented world generation for temperature, elevation, hilliness, and rainfall
[*]Less flat land and more hills/mountains
[*]More frequent small-to-medium rivers instead of continental-scale rivers
[*]More complex coastlines with additional bays, peninsulas, and islands
[*]Four environment bands: warm-temperate forest / cool-temperate forest / subalpine forest / alpine zone
[*]Sudajii, Japanese beech, Shirabiso, and Haimatsu as structural representative plants
[*]Natural soil balance with progressively poorer/stony ground at higher elevations
[*]Uses Vanilla systems for rain, fog, snow, fall color, and leafless winter states wherever possible
[/list]

Rather than replacing everything with parallel systems, the mod is designed to reuse RimWorld 1.6 world generation, River / Coast mutators, weather, snow, and deciduous-plant behavior whenever possible.

[h2]Using CCTO[/h2]

[b]Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul (CCTO) is optional.[/b]

With AMJE alone, its plants keep temperature behavior closer to the Vanilla baseline.

When CCTO is also active, AMJE's own plants receive species-specific minimum growth temperatures plus cold death or dormancy behavior, creating a [b]more realism-focused cold-climate model[/b].

In short:
[list]
[*][b]AMJE alone[/b]: a medieval-Japan-like natural environment
[*][b]AMJE + CCTO[/b]: the same environment with stricter plant cold tolerance
[/list]

CCTO is not required; AMJE remains complete as a standalone environment mod.

[h2]Medieval Overhaul[/h2]

Medieval Overhaul is also optional, but normal AMJ play is expected to use both.

AMJE does not globally replace MO's natural biome/terrain identity. Specialized content such as Dark Forest is preserved where compatible.

[h2]Dependencies[/h2]

[b]Required:[/b] Harmony

[b]Optional:[/b] Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul / Medieval Overhaul

Ancient & Medieval Japan Core is not required.

[h2]Save Compatibility[/h2]

[b]A new game is recommended to evaluate the intended world generation, biomes, rivers, coastlines, and terrain.[/b]

Adding AMJE to an existing save has not been verified and does not regenerate an existing world. Some runtime systems, such as temperature behavior, may still be affected.

[b]Removing AMJE from a save that uses its custom biomes, terrain, or plants is not recommended.[/b]

[h2]Development Status[/h2]

Currently Beta.

The main environment systems and [b]custom artwork for Sudajii, Japanese beech, Shirabiso and Haimatsu[/b] are implemented, including snow overlays and beech leafless/autumn states. Retextures of existing Vanilla/MO trees are planned for post-release updates.

See the GitHub README / Design documentation for detailed values, rationale, and CCTO integration data.

AI assistance is used for code implementation, documentation, research, and test development. Design, balance decisions, and final testing/review are performed by the author.
