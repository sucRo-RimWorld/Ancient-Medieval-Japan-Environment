[h1]Ancient & Medieval Japan - Environment（中世日本 - 環境）[/h1]

[b]Reconfigures Vanilla terrain, climate, vegetation, wildlife and biomes for a medieval Japan-inspired landscape.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adjust temperature, rainfall, elevation and hilliness; reweight rain, fog, thunderstorms and snow by climate zone and wetland
[*]Increase small and medium rivers while suppressing natural huge rivers; make coasts more irregular with bays, peninsulas and islands
[*]Add four biomes: warm-temperate forest, cool-temperate forest, subalpine forest and alpine zone
[*]Add Thin Soil (50% fertility), with more stony/poor natural soil at high elevation
[*]Add four plants: Sudajii, Japanese beech, Shirabiso and Haimatsu; correct their cut-wood yields
[*]Exclude Vanilla vegetation absent from medieval Japan, redistribute trees/grasses and limit tree-sowing choices by region; Haimatsu is unsowable
[*]Retain TemperateSwamp and ColdBog while adjusting their wild-plant pools; keep Vanilla wetland mud, marsh, shallow water and movement difficulty
[*]Rebalance wildlife with selected Vanilla animal proxies; exclude unsuitable wetland pack animals
[*]Adjust wetland disease types and intervals; exclude mechanites
[*]Revise descriptions of both wetlands and six retained Vanilla trees with medieval-Japan information
[/list]

[h2]Scope[/h2]
RimWorld 1.6, Beta. Terrain, climate, rivers, coasts, soil, plants, wildlife, weather and diseases. English / Japanese.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[b]Required:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url]
[b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul (CCTO)[/url]
Species-specific cold tolerances for representative plants when CCTO is enabled.
[b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2553700067]Medieval Overhaul[/url]
Coexists with its Dark Forest and other specialized biomes.
Existing Healroot and herbal medicine remain available. Medicinal plants are outside Environment.

[h2]Save compatibility[/h2]
[b]New game recommended[/b] because world generation changes. Adding to existing saves is unverified and does not rebuild existing worlds. Runtime temperature changes may affect existing saves. [b]Do not remove AMJE[/b] from saves using its custom biomes, terrain or plants.

[h2]日本語 / Japanese[/h2]

[b]バニラの地形・気候・植生・動物・バイオーム構成を中世の日本列島向けに再構成する環境MOD。[/b]

[h2]主な特徴[/h2]
[list]
[*]気温・降水量・標高・丘陵・山地の分布と、気候帯・湿地ごとの天候（雨・霧・雷雨・積雪）を調整
[*]小～中規模の河川を増やし、巨大河川の自然発生を抑制。湾・半島・島が多い複雑な海岸線へ変更
[*]暖温帯林・冷温帯林・亜高山帯林・高山帯の4バイオームを追加
[*]痩せた土壌（肥沃度50%）を追加し、高地ほど礫地・痩せた土が増えるよう調整
[*]スダジイ・ブナ・シラビソ・ハイマツの4植物を追加し、伐採時の木材取得を調整
[*]中世日本に存在しないバニラ植生を除外し、樹木・草本の出現比率と地域別の植林候補を調整。ハイマツは植林不可
[*]温帯湿地・冷涼湿原を維持し、自然植生を調整。泥・沼・浅瀬・移動困難度はバニラを維持
[*]野生動物の出現構成を調整し、湿地では中世日本に存在しない荷役動物候補を除外
[*]湿地の病気の種類・発生間隔を調整し、機械虫病を除外
[*]既存バニラ樹木6種と湿地2種の説明文を、中世日本に関する情報を含めて修正
[/list]

[h2]対応範囲[/h2]
RimWorld 1.6対応、β版。地形・気候・河川・海岸・土壌・植物・動物・天候・疾病が対象。日本語 / 英語。

[h2]対応・互換性[/h2]
[b]必須MOD:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url]
[b]任意MOD:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]CCTO[/url]
CCTO併用時は代表植物に種類別の寒冷耐性を適用。
[b]任意MOD:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2553700067]Medieval Overhaul[/url]
冥い森などの特殊バイオームとは共存。
既存Healrootと医薬品供給は維持。薬用植物はEnvironmentの対象外。

[h2]セーブ互換性[/h2]
ワールド生成を変更しているため[b]新規ゲームを推奨[/b]。既存セーブへの追加は未検証で、生成済みワールドは変わらない。温度などの実行時処理は既存セーブにも影響する可能性がある。独自バイオーム・地形・植物を使うセーブからの[b]途中削除は非推奨[/b]。
