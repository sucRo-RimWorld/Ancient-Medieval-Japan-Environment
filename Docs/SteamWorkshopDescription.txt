[h1]Ancient & Medieval Japan - Environment[/h1]

[b]A standalone environment overhaul that replaces and reconfigures Vanilla terrain, vegetation, and biome composition for a Japanese-archipelago-inspired landscape.[/b]

It creates an ancient-to-medieval-Japan-inspired environment with a humid climate, mountainous terrain, frequent short rivers, complex coastlines, temperature-based forest belts, and alpine zones.

[b]If you only want a medieval-Japan-like natural environment, this mod can be used on its own.[/b]

[h2]Main Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Japan-oriented world generation for temperature, elevation, hilliness, and rainfall
[*]Less flat land and more hills/mountains
[*]More frequent small-to-medium rivers instead of continental-scale rivers
[*]More complex coastlines with additional bays, peninsulas, and islands
[*]Four environment bands: warm-temperate forest / cool-temperate forest / subalpine forest / alpine zone
[*]Sudajii, Japanese beech, Shirabiso, and Haimatsu as structural representative plants
[*]Natural soil balance with progressively poorer/stony ground at higher elevations
[*]Uses Vanilla systems for rain, fog, snow, fall color, and leafless winter states wherever possible
[/list]

After existing WorldGen, AMJE reconfigures terrain, climate, and biome placement. Local rivers/coasts use existing River / Coast mutators; weather, snow, and leafless behavior also reuse Vanilla systems.

[h2]Why These Four Environment Bands?[/h2]

AMJE's biomes are not intended to reproduce modern prefectural or regional borders. Using Japan's vegetation classification as a reference, the mod simplifies the [b]major vegetation bands that change with temperature and elevation[/b] into four RimWorld-friendly stages.

[list]
[*][b]Warm-temperate forest[/b]: evergreen broadleaf lucidophyll forest
[*][b]Cool-temperate forest[/b]: deciduous broadleaf forest such as beech woodland
[*][b]Subalpine forest[/b]: evergreen conifer forest such as Shirabiso woodland
[*][b]Alpine zone[/b]: Haimatsu scrub and alpine vegetation above the treeline
[/list]

These four added biomes take precedence over broad Vanilla biomes on eligible land as [b]Japan-oriented baseline vegetation bands[/b]. Wetlands and specialized biomes such as [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2553700067]Medieval Overhaul[/url]'s Dark Forest can still coexist when their conditions are met.

The biome mixes also avoid presenting every area as untouched single-species climax forest. Historical vegetation research indicates that human activity from the Yayoi period through the medieval period increased pine woodland, grassland, and secondary forest in many settled regions, so Vanilla trees and ground vegetation remain as secondary components.

[h2]Representative Plant for Each Environment Band[/h2]

Each band adds [b]one representative structural plant[/b] so its landscape can be read at a glance. These were not chosen simply as "famous medieval trees." The criterion is whether the plant represents Japan's natural vegetation and can express a landscape connected to ancient and medieval Japan.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/main/Textures/Things/Plant/AMJ/Shii/Shii_A.png[/img]
[b]Warm-temperate forest — Sudajii[/b]
A tall evergreen tree representative of the lucidophyll forests of southern Honshu, Shikoku, and Kyushu. Shii trees have long formed part of southwestern Japan's forests; their seeds were eaten, their wood used for buildings and implements, and bark tannin used for dyeing.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/main/Textures/Things/Plant/AMJ/Beech/Beech_A.png[/img]
[b]Cool-temperate forest — Japanese beech[/b]
A large deciduous tree representative of Japan's cool-temperate forests. Examples from the late Heian through medieval period show Fagus wood being selectively used for turned wooden objects such as lacquerware.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/main/Textures/Things/Plant/AMJ/Shirabiso/Shirabiso_A.png[/img]
[b]Subalpine forest — Shirabiso[/b]
A tall evergreen conifer representative of Honshu's subalpine zone. Mountains such as Mt. Ontake carry forests of Shirabiso and related conifers, forming part of the mountain landscape that became a setting for ascetic practice and religious ascent by the later medieval period.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Environment/main/Textures/Things/Plant/AMJ/Haimatsu/Haimatsu_A.png[/img]
[b]Alpine zone — Haimatsu[/b]
A dwarf pine representative of terrain above the treeline. It forms dense creeping scrub and helps create a distinctly Japanese alpine landscape. AMJE deliberately avoids filling the alpine band with ordinary tall trees, so the treeline difference is visible both in scenery and available timber.

The in-game descriptions provide additional details on kanji names, aliases, distribution, and each plant's relationship to ancient and medieval Japan.

[h2]Using CCTO[/h2]

[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul (CCTO)[/url] is optional.[/b]

With AMJE alone, its plants keep temperature behavior closer to the Vanilla baseline.

When CCTO is also active, AMJE's own plants receive species-specific minimum growth temperatures plus cold death or dormancy behavior, creating a [b]more realism-focused cold-climate model[/b].

In short:
[list]
[*][b]AMJE alone[/b]: a medieval-Japan-like natural environment
[*][b]AMJE + CCTO[/b]: the same environment with stricter plant cold tolerance
[/list]

CCTO is not required; AMJE remains complete as a standalone environment mod.

[h2]Medieval Overhaul[/h2]

Medieval Overhaul is also optional, but normal AMJ play is expected to use both.

AMJE does not globally replace MO's natural biome/terrain identity. Specialized content such as Dark Forest is preserved where compatible.

[h2]Save Compatibility[/h2]

[b]A new game is recommended to evaluate the intended world generation, biomes, rivers, coastlines, and terrain.[/b]

Adding AMJE to an existing save has not been verified and does not regenerate an existing world. Some runtime systems, such as temperature behavior, may still be affected.

[b]Removing AMJE from a save that uses its custom biomes, terrain, or plants is not recommended.[/b]

The main environment systems are implemented, and [b]Sudajii, Japanese beech, Shirabiso, and Haimatsu now use AMJE-authored custom graphics[/b], including snow overlays and beech leafless/autumn states.
